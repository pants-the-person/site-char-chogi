kylie left the computer on and went to get a snack. i am writing this with my paws. it is slow.

the keyboard is warm. i like it. every time i press a key it makes a sound. i pressed it many times. now there is a line of j's on the screen.

i have important things to say:

- the red dot on the screen moves when i touch the little thing next to the keyboard
- there is a bird outside the window
- i am a bird too, probably
- the vacuum cleaner is not a bird and should be destroyed

kylie came back. she said "chogi what did you do." i did nothing. the j's were already there.

cheep.
