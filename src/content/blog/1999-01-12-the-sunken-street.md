I am told a personal page should begin with an introduction. Here is mine: I was abandoned in the alleys of Ul'dah before I had a name. The city gave me one eventually. It was not kind.

Ul'dah is sandstone and sharp elbows. I learned both before I could read. I was a Lalafell with horns and scales—an anomaly, which is a polite word for a target. The bigger urchins made sure I understood that. I was too small to win a fight, and too stubborn to die from one.

So I learned a different trade. **Leverage.**

A secret is worth more than a coin. A favor is worth more than a blade. A boy who knows which merchant is cheating which creditor can walk through alleys where bigger boys cannot. By my teens I had organized the city's orphan Lalafells into the **Sinker & Line**. We were part family, part mafia, and entirely mine. We controlled the back-alley trade and the kind of information that never makes it into a ledger. The Brass Blades never saw us because they were looking for a gang, and we looked like a family of very small beggars.

To keep it that way, I took up fishing.

The Thanalan coast is good for it. The docks are better. Some men pray. I cast. A small angler with a tackle box can stand in one place for hours and nobody asks why. He can watch the tides, meet a contact, and learn which ship is carrying what. If a man can fish, he will never starve. If he can fish in front of witnesses, he will never be suspected.

The barrels were salted fish on the manifest. They were not always salted fish in the hold. I will leave it at that. The Brass Blades read manifests. The Sinker & Line read the spaces between them.

That was the beginning. Everything after this was ambition.

If you are reading this on the old net, welcome. If you are reading it because someone gave you the address, I hope you brought a warrant and a good pair of boots.
