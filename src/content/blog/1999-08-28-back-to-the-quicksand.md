I stepped back onto Eorzean soil and discovered that years had passed while I was gone. The Xaela bodyguard I had grown fond of was long gone, vanished into the shifting history of the realm. I did not have time to grieve. I had a life to reclaim and a syndicate to reunite.

The Sinker & Line had kept the lights on. They had also kept the books. I checked.

Now I hold court from a quiet corner of **The Quicksand**. It is not the most comfortable table. It is the one with the best sightlines. I listen to tavern rumors the way I used to listen to dockside manifests. The current rumor is the Warrior of Light, returned from a world flooded with blinding light. The First, they call it. A place of primordial day.

I watch dark void-aether spark across my knuckles and wonder.

Traveling there might finally quell the black magic. Bathing my soul in that much light could burn the taint out of me, or burn the rest of me with it. It might also make me stronger. It might make me nothing at all.

It is a high-stakes gamble. I have made a career of those.

The bait doesn't care who's holding the rod, and neither do I. We all get hooked eventually.

If you see me at the Quicksand, do not ask for a reading. Buy me a rum and I will tell you a lie that is more useful.
