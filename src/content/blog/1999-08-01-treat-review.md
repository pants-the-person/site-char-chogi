today i reviewed three treats. this is a serious job and i took it seriously.

**tuna crunchies — 5 paws**

good. crunchy. smell like fish. i would like another one.

**the green ones — 1 paw**

bad. they taste like grass. i ate it anyway because it was on the floor. i regret it.

**kylie's toast crust — 4 paws**

not technically a treat. still good. she said "that is my breakfast." i said nothing.

**a moth — 3 paws**

i caught it. it was crunchy in a different way. i do not think i should eat another one.

overall a good week for treats. i would like more tuna crunchies. if you are reading this, tell kylie.
