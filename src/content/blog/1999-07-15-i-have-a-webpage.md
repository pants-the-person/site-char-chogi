kylie says i am famous now.

she made me a webpage. there is a picture of me on it (it is a drawing because the scanner is broken). i have a blog. i have a guestbook. i have a hit counter.

i do not know what a hit counter is. i think it counts the hits. i have hit many things. the mouse. the plant. the vacuum cleaner (i lost).

people can leave me messages. i cannot read. kylie reads them to me. she says people think i am cute. i already knew that.

if you visit my page, sign my guestbook. kylie will read it to me. i will cheep in approval.

now i am going to sit on the warm monitor.
